05.My vision for the future

While technology forms the structural backbone of digital transformation
, human adaptability remains its most critical operational factor.
Implementing cutting-edge digital platforms often triggers friction within an
organization due to employee resistance, skill gaps, or fear of job redundancy.
Overcoming these hurdles requires strong, empathetic leadership and a dedicated commitment
to continuous upskilling. Management must cultivate a corporate culture that values continuous learning,
trial-and-error experimentation, and open communication. Providing practical training programs
and encouraging cross-departmental collaboration helps workforce members view technology as a tool
that enhances their capabilities rather than replaces them, ensuring a smooth and sustainable digital shift.
